How to play

  • Place one boxer in every row, every column and every colored region.
  • 🚫 Boxers need their space. They can’t touch each other, not even diagonally.
  • Tap a square once to mark it with a paw (no boxer here). Tap again to place a boxer, and a third time to clear it.
  • 👆 Drag across squares to mark lots of them at once.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and you can always solve it with logic. No guessing needed.
